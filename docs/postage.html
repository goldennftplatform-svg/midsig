    What does one stamp cover?+

    One authenticated message to one envelope recipient. A message to three recipients uses three stamps. The planned price is 0.05 USDC per stamp, with no bundle discount that weakens the minimum. The payment ledger and paid SMTP enforcement are still being built.

    Why buy a book instead of one stamp?+

    One deposit can fund many messages, spreading transaction costs across your postage. Once implemented, the gateway will debit credits automatically when accepting a valid signed message. You won't need to approve a wallet transaction for every email.

    Where do payments go?+

    MidSig keeps fiat and crypto settlement separate. Card payments are processed in dollars through Square. Native USDC payments settle directly on Base.

    Direct wallet transfers are not postage purchases unless they are attached to a MidSig order. Card-payment receipts are hashed locally, batched into a Merkle commitment, and anchored to Base without publishing card details on-chain. Read the payment architecture →

    LIVE · PROVEN ON BASE

    Email postage with a cryptographic receipt.

    MidSig is no longer a payment mockup. The production gateway signs the message, binds the actual SMTP recipients and message content, charges postage, and can independently anchor fiat-settlement receipts to Base.

    Domain-bound identity

    An Ed25519 MidSig key published by the sending domain proves which domain authorized the message instead of trusting the visible From line alone.

    Content + recipient binding

    MidSig v2 commits to the message body and SMTP-envelope recipients. Changing the content or replaying the signature toward another recipient breaks verification.

    Paid SMTP admission

    Valid postage is charged per recipient only after cryptographic verification. A valid signature without sufficient postage does not buy delivery.

    Fiat receipts anchored on-chain

    Successful card-payment receipts stay private server-side. Their hashes are combined into deterministic Merkle batches and committed to Base using a dedicated gas wallet.
